Work icons: 100 for the vocabulary picker

Each icon at 16 px and 24 px, on the light and the dark canvas. 36 are existing PAIMOS drawings, kept as they are; 64 are new, from Lucide 1.55.0 (ISC; see NOTICE), and sit on a faintly tinted canvas.

The new ones use Lucide path data scaled from its 24 grid onto the PAIMOS 16 grid: stroke 1.8, round caps and joins, no fill, current colour. 14 that clogged at 16 px were simplified: file-text, folder-kanban, gem, goal, handshake, megaphone, notebook-pen, package-check, palette, pen-tool, siren, target, telescope, trophy.

The first row repeats six PAIMOS icons so the new ones can be judged against them. The order is the order in the picker.
